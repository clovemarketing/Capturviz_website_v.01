Why teams start with a scan

Existing conditions you can measure

Drawings go out of date. A scan records the space as it stands today, so designers and contractors work from the same reference instead of assumptions.

Capture combines 3D spatial data with panoramic imagery, so you can measure and also see what’s there.

Five stages, with the scope and deliverables agreed before anyone arrives on site.

    Tell us about the space and the outcome you need.

  2. Capture

    We scan on site with mobile equipment.

  3. Process

    Data is registered, cleaned and checked.

  4. Model

    We produce the models, plans or twin you ordered.

  5. Deliver

    You receive the files and a handover summary.
