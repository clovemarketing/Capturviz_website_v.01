Technology & deliverables

What we capture, how it’s processed and what you can receive at the end.

Request a scanSee how it works

Capture

Mobile scanning records millions of 3D points as we move through the space, together with panoramic imagery. Mobile capture covers large and complex areas efficiently.

Processing

Scans are registered into a single coordinate system, cleaned of noise and checked for coverage against the agreed scope.

Production

From the processed data we build the models, drawings, twins or comparisons in your scope, then check them before handover.

Built to open in your tools

Point cloud formats such as E57 are widely supported by design and BIM software, and our Scan-to-BIM models are built for Revit and other leading BIM platforms. Tell us your software and version so we can match it.

Accuracy, stated plainly

Accuracy depends on the equipment, the environment and what the data is for. Rather than promising a single figure for every job, we agree an accuracy target with you before capture and record it with the delivered data.
