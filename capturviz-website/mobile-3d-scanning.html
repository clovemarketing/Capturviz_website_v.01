Mobile 3D scanning

We walk the space with mobile scanning equipment and record dense 3D point data alongside panoramic imagery. It’s a fast way to document existing conditions, including complex and hard-to-reach areas.

Request this serviceDeliverables & formats

What it’s for

  • Recording existing conditions before design or renovation
  • As-built records at handover
  • Plant rooms, ceilings and congested service areas
  • Creating the base data for BIM, plans or a digital twin

What you receive

  • Registered point cloud of the captured area
  • Panoramic imagery linked to the scan
  • A capture summary noting coverage and any limits

File formats and detail level are confirmed in your scope.

A good fit when

  • Your drawings are missing, old or unreliable
  • You need to document a large area with minimal disruption
  • Several teams need to work from one shared record

Questions

How accurate is the scan?

Accuracy depends on the equipment, the environment and the deliverable. We agree an accuracy target with you before capture and document it with the results, rather than quoting one number for every project.

Does the building need to be empty?

Not necessarily. We plan capture around occupancy and access. Tell us about restricted areas, working hours and safety requirements when you request a scan.

How long does capture take?

It depends on the size, layout and level of detail. Your quote includes an estimated time on site.
